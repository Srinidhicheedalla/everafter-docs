# How to Login to EverAfter

## Overview

This guide explains how users can log in
to their EverAfter account.

## Prerequisites

- A registered EverAfter account.
- A valid email address.
- Your account password.

## Steps to Login

1. Open the EverAfter application.
2. Click the Login button.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Submit.

## Expected Result

The user should successfully log in
and access their account.
